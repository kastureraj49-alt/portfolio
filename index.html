KNOW THE RISKS

Common online threats.

Learn the warning signs so you can make safer choices online.

✉

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites try to trick you into sharing passwords, OTPs, or bank details.

Watch for urgent language and strange links.
🔑

Weak Passwords

Short or reused passwords make it easier for someone to access multiple accounts.

Use long, unique passwords for each account.
⚠

Online Scams

Fraudsters may promise prizes, jobs, refunds, or deals to pressure you into paying or sharing data.

Verify the offer through an official channel.
📶

Unsafe Networks

Public Wi-Fi can expose you to risks, especially when visiting untrusted sites or sharing sensitive data.

Avoid sensitive transactions on unknown networks.

MAKE SECURITY A HABIT

Small steps. Stronger safety.

Use this checklist whenever you go online.

01

Pause before you click

Check the sender, domain name, spelling, and the actual destination of a link. If unsure, visit the official site yourself.

02

Never share OTPs or passwords

Legitimate support staff should not ask you to reveal your password or one-time verification code.

03

Use unique passwords and MFA

Use a password manager if possible, and enable multi-factor authentication on important accounts.

04

Update your devices

Install updates from trusted sources. Updates often fix security weaknesses in apps and operating systems.

05

Back up important files

Keep a separate backup of important work and photos so you can recover them if a device is lost or damaged.

!

IF SOMETHING FEELS WRONG

Stop. Don't panic.

  1. Don't reply, pay, or click further.
  2. Change affected passwords using the official website.
  3. Tell your bank or service provider if financial details were shared.
  4. In India, report cybercrime through official government channels.

This educational project is not a live threat scanner or emergency response service.
